# Code of conduct

We want participation in StyxRouter to be welcoming and productive. Be respectful, assume good faith, accept constructive feedback, and focus on the work. Different perspectives and levels of experience are welcome.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable. These expectations apply in project spaces and when representing the project.

Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or take other proportionate action. To report conduct concerns privately, contact a maintainer through the repository profile. Include relevant links and context; do not post sensitive personal information in public issues. Maintainers should handle reports discreetly and avoid involving anyone named in a report in its review.
